# Inferno and weapon arsenal

Inferno Caldera follows Celestial Summit. Its gate costs 1,000,000 scroll coins. A hollow basalt volcano surrounds a walk-in lava cavern with a level entrance causeway. The mountain chain now ends beyond the last configured zone, and side mountains stay outside the wider final zone.

The cavern has five personal Ember Oni (levels 45–55), double their normal health, glowing red bodies, three decorative lava pools, eleven interior treasure ledges and a safe rest dojo. Lava is atmospheric, not an additional damage hazard. Gold katana owners retain the advertised one-hit enemy benefit.

Dojo weapon unlocks: Kunai 250, Shuriken 1,500, Nunchaku 4,000, Bow and Arrow 10,000 scroll coins. Unlocks save with the existing player data and restore tools on respawn. Equip through the Roblox backpack. Thrown weapons and arrows have unlimited ammunition. Weapon Power is the renamed Katana Power upgrade and preserves existing levels; it strengthens all weapons. Katana remains the chest-opening weapon.

Bow: 80-stud range, 1s cooldown, 3.5× power. Shuriken: 40 studs, .35s, .85×. Kunai: 28 studs, .45s, 1.15×. Nunchaku: 8 studs, .35s, 1.8×. Attacks aim at the closest personal enemy; only a clear line of sight deals damage. The server checks ownership, equipped tool, alive state, menu immunity and a shared attack clock. Projectile travel is a visual effect; damage resolves at firing time.

Validation: Luau compilation, regression suites, Rojo build. Studio visual/mobile playtesting and publishing remain necessary. In Studio, walk from Spirit through Sky and Celestial, unlock Inferno at the doorway, enter/exit the cavern, test the rest dojo, purchase/equip each weapon, test walls and menu immunity, then respawn and rejoin with saving enabled.
